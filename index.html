    Ask questions of my PDF pile. Answers cite the page they came from.

  • Fifty Questions

    Fifty questions with automatic checkers. Four small models ran it on my laptop and scored from 29 to 47. Every one of them struggled most with following instructions.

    Running

    Python, Transformers, no GPU

  • Door Cat

  • Prompt Compost

    A log of prompts that failed, what went wrong, and the fix that finally worked.

    Sprouting

  • Memo Sorter

    Tags a short note as idea, todo, reminder or journal. It gets 40 of 48 unseen notes right and still mixes up ideas and todos.

    Running

    22M-parameter MiniLM, trained on a laptop CPU in 36 seconds
